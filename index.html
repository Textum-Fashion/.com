Welcome to Textum Fashion Ltd.

Textum Fashion Ltd. stands at the forefront of the denim garment industry in Bangladesh, delivering premium quality products tailored for men, women, and children. With a commitment to innovation, Textum Fashion Ltd. ensures the highest standards in denim manufacturing, making it a trusted name in global fashion markets.

What Do You Know About Us?

Textum Fashion Ltd. is a modern and technologically developed project of denim and woven products, exporting to buyers all over the world. It is a sister concern of the local giant Buying House Textum Global.

The brand new machinery for 2 lines of garments has been imported from a reputed manufacturer of different countries, and the production is running smoothly. The product mix and annual production capacity of the project are explained in this report.

Textum Fashion Ltd. is fully equipped with state-of-the-art automatic and semi-automatic machinery. With a total project area of 8,054 sft, the entire factory facility ensures that workers are motivated, energised and safe to deliver world-class products. Currently, Textum Fashion Ltd. is ensuring the livelihood of about 200 people who work together as a team to make the organisation a success.

Quality Procedure

Every order passes these checkpoints, from fabric to final inspection.

  1. Standard AQL System is maintained in every step by the highly qualified, trained and independent Quality Team who report to the management directly.
  2. MS policy is maintained.
  3. Product safety policy is maintained by separate team and expertise.
  4. Buyer required that AQL is maintained for every individual order.
  5. 4 Points fabrics inspection system is maintained.
  6. Fabrics roll wise color shade, weight, shrinkage and wash test and overall quality inspection procedure is followed.
  7. Trims & accessories checklist and inspection is maintained.
  8. Bulk size set and wash test is mandatory before proceeding bulk run.
  9. PP meeting between buyer representatives and factory team is mandatory before starting bulk, trial run.
  10. Cutting pcs and panel checking/inspection system is maintained.
  11. Sewing process quality inspection and in-line inspection system is maintained.
  12. Table QC checking system before moving to washing/finishing.
  13. Traffic light system is maintained in 7 pcs.
  14. Quality inspection before iron & after ironing is strongly maintained.
  15. Daily production & quality meeting system is followed to discuss and fix all quality issues of running production.
  16. Random inspection done by the individual QA team on daily packed goods.
  17. Pre-final done by trained QA team before offering final random inspection.
